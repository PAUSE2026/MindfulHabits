01 · YOUR PRIVACY, EXPLAINED SIMPLY

What happens to my information?

Who made this? Mindful Habits is created by MentorHealth Philippines. For questions about privacy, use App Help & Feedback to prepare your message. Only use verified contact channels when communicating with the team.

What information can I choose to enter?

A companion name/color, selected role, care-card favorites, habits and routine progress; mood choices, a custom feeling, and diary reflections you decide to write. Please avoid full names, addresses, passwords, school IDs, or another person's private information in free-text areas.

Why is the information used?

To personalize your companion, display your selected habits, and show a diary/calendar history. The app is not designed to diagnose a condition, assess risk, rank students, or report emotions to the school.

Where is it kept, and for how long?

In this browser's local storage, preferences, favorites and routines remain until removed or browser storage is cleared. Diary entries remain in this browser until you delete them or browser storage is cleared. Closing a tab does not necessarily clear unsaved session notes until the page is reloaded. There is no personal cloud account or automatic backup. If your browser data is cleared or you change devices, your entries may be lost.

Storage and privacy: Local browser storage is not encrypted by this application. Anyone with access to the same browser profile or device may be able to read or remove saved information. Browser extensions, device administrators, and browser or device backups may introduce additional risks. Use a personal, trusted device, or do not save sensitive reflections.

Who can see it?

The app does not automatically send diary entries to school staff, peer supporters, parents, or MentorHealth. The application itself does not send personal entries to a MentorHealth server. However, your browser/device may expose locally stored data to people with access. Opening third-party resources or font services may involve independent network requests governed by those providers' privacy practices. For sensitive experiences, consider speaking to someone you trust rather than relying on this device as a private vault.

What are my choices and rights?

You may skip sensitive questions, delete entries, erase local records, and ask questions about privacy. Under applicable Philippine data-protection law, individuals have rights including information, access, correction and other rights subject to legal conditions. For privacy questions, you can prepare a message under App Help & Feedback; this page does not send it for you.

Children, parents, caregivers and school use

Children deserve clear explanations and additional protection, not a blanket promise that adults can never be involved. Young users deserve age-appropriate explanations, appropriate adult guidance, and clear safeguarding boundaries. A welcome acknowledgment alone does not authorize schools or other people to see private entries.

02 · USING THIS SPACE WITH CARE

Using Mindful Habits with care

What this app offers

Optional feelings check-ins, daily reflections, guided general-wellbeing activities, customizable habits, care cards, companion personalization, and information about support pathways.

What this app does not offer

It does not provide professional therapy, diagnosis, medical advice, counseling, emergency response, continuous monitoring, case reporting, or a verified peer-support chat. No one is automatically alerted about anything written here. If you are in danger or need urgent assistance, reach a trusted adult or an appropriate emergency/protection service directly.

How to use it respectfully

Use it for your own wellbeing. Avoid entering other people's personal information or using it to harm, threaten, embarrass or monitor someone. Do not rely on it as your only source of help. You can stop an activity, leave a question unanswered, or close the app at any time.

Accuracy, changes and availability

Information and support contacts can change; always confirm availability with the service. The app may be updated, and entries kept only in your browser can be lost if you clear data or switch devices.

Agreement is not consent to everything

Accepting these terms does not authorize sharing your diary with others or remote monitoring. Any future sharing or monitoring feature would require separate safeguards and a lawful basis.

03 · SAFEGUARDING & TRUST

What a safe space means here

Care by Design: simple words, readable pages, no shame-based rewards, privacy-minded defaults and meaningful choices. Caring for Carers: students, teachers and caregivers can find appropriate support without recording somebody else's case in a personal diary.

Peer supporters: a supervised support pathway, separate from an app chat. Peer supporters must have clear boundaries, training and referral procedures; they must never have automatic access to diaries.

When safety is at risk: use the for urgent or personal concerns. For app bugs, unsafe content, privacy concerns or partnerships, use . The latter creates a copyable draft only. It does not send or log a report.

Accountable support: Support must involve real, accountable people with clear responsibilities, verified contact details, and child-sensitive referral procedures. No diary entry here is automatically received as a safeguarding report.

04 · HOW WE KEEP CARE RESPONSIBLE

Our safeguarding guidelines

A safe space is not only a calming design. It means knowing your choices, understanding boundaries, and being able to reach real people when you need support.

For school concerns, DepEd Order No. 40, s. 2012 provides the Child Protection Policy. DepEd Order No. 006, s. 2026 sets out updated guidelines for safe and motivating learning environments, including learner rights protection. The official DepEd learner protection reporting page also offers an option to submit learner rights concerns. These services are outside Mindful Habits and are not automatically contacted by the app.

Privacy, diary and personal choice
  • Feelings, journals, and routines are for your personal reflection. They are not school behavior scores or attendance records.
  • Only write what feels safe to keep on this device. Avoid names or identifying details about yourself or others.
  • Your diary is not automatically shared with teachers, caregivers, peer supporters, or the MentorHealth team.
  • On a shared device, others using your browser profile may be able to see entries. Browser storage is not encrypted. Delete saved content when needed and avoid sensitive writing on a shared device.
  • You can skip an activity, stop a breathing exercise, or delete saved entries without losing access to care.
Bullying, discrimination and unsafe behavior
  • You deserve to be treated with dignity at school, at home, and online. Harmful jokes, public shaming, threats, harassment and discrimination are not your responsibility to manage alone.
  • For personal concerns, seek a trained guidance professional, designated child protection person, trusted adult, or appropriate independent service.
  • If the concern involves the first adult you might approach, choose a different trusted adult or an independent child protection service. You do not need to confront the person involved.
  • Saving something in your diary does not submit a report. Formal reporting should use an approved school or child protection channel with clear follow-up procedures.
Peer supporters and trusted adults
  • A peer supporter may listen respectfully and help you find adult or professional support. They are not a therapist, investigator, or crisis responder.
  • Peer supporters must not ask for passwords, diary screenshots, secret conversations, or promises to keep a safety concern hidden.
  • Any organized peer-support program needs selection and training, a safeguarding focal person, supervision, referral boundaries and a way to raise concerns about a supporter.
  • Support is not limited to a peer supporter. You can go directly to guidance staff, a trusted adult, child protection personnel or an outside service.
Distress, difficult memories and immediate danger
  • Reflection prompts are optional. If a memory feels upsetting, stop writing and take a break, move somewhere safer, or seek someone you trust.
  • Mimo can remind you of caring steps, but cannot recognize emergencies, monitor diary text, or contact anyone on your behalf.
  • If you or someone else is in immediate danger, do not wait for an app response. In the Philippines, call 911 or approach local emergency services.
  • For child protection concerns, MAKABATA 1383 is a national help route. For a mental health crisis, NCMH 1553 is another option.
Feedback, accountability and app concerns
  • Use App Help & Feedback for broken buttons, inaccessible text, privacy questions, unsafe app content or suggestions.
  • Feedback is prepared for copying. It is not sent, received, tracked or answered inside Mindful Habits. Do not put a sensitive child protection disclosure into the feedback field.
  • Responsible implementation requires a named safeguarding lead, an accessible and independently available complaint route, current contact details, clear response responsibilities, and regular review with young people.
  • A concern involving an app team member or school staff should have an alternative route that does not depend on that person.

How do I ask for help?

You can say: “Something happened at school. I need help and I want to understand what will happen next.”

You do not have to describe everything at once. You can ask how your information will be handled. Where a child's safety is at risk, the person helping may need to involve authorized protection personnel.

If one route does not feel safe or does not help, you can choose another trusted person or independent reporting channel. You do not need to enter your story into the diary.
