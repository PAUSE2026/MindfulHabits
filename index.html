My boundaries and support

You can care deeply without carrying everything alone.

Privacy comes first.

This is not a student case log, clinical supervision record, or confidential incident-reporting tool. Avoid entering student names, identifying details, or descriptions of protected cases. For safeguarding and workplace concerns, use your institution's approved procedures or independent services.

COMMUNITY CARE · SYSTEMS OF CARE

Care within reach.

The same app can help students, school staff and community members understand which services are available around them. Start with reviewed information; don't guess contacts or hours.

LOCAL FIRST · NATIONAL WHEN NEEDED

Find the right person or service.

A referral directory should explain what a service does, who it serves, how to reach it, and whether the information is verified. Help is not a sequence you must complete—urgent support is available directly.

Community Care Packs

Future locally verified packs, rather than a new app for every school or barangay.

Help Me Say It

Sometimes the first step is knowing what to say. Choose an example, adapt it, then copy it only if you want.

Nothing is sent automatically. Copying or sharing is always your choice.

For partner schools and communities

Each community pack would need a responsible editor, approved content, last-verification dates, and a process for correcting information. Student notes and mood answers must not be accessible through this directory.

No live reporting, appointments, case tracking, or institutional integration is included in this prototype.

REAL PEOPLE · REAL SUPPORT

You don't have to do everything alone.

A clear path to school, community, city and national support. This app is not a counseling or emergency-response service.

Need help now?

For immediate danger in the Philippines call 911. For child protection concerns, MAKABATA 1383. For mental health crisis support, NCMH 1553. Service availability should be reverified before any school pilot.
